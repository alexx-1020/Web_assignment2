Weekend hikes in the mountains of Lebanon

Every Saturday, Jurd takes a small group from Beirut to one of the country's best trails. We drive, a local guide leads the way, and lunch is in a village guesthouse.

Book a hike See the trails

Our trails

Three routes, from an easy forest walk to a full day in the high mountains.

Easy

Chouf Cedars

A shaded walk under old cedar trees. About 6 km and 3 hours.

Moderate

Qadisha Valley

Down into the valley, past monasteries built into the rock. About 11 km and 5 hours.

Hard

Qornet es-Sawda

A long climb to the highest peak in Lebanon. About 14 km and 7 hours.
